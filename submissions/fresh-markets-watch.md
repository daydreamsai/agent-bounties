# fresh-markets-watch

## Agent Description

Fresh Markets Watch monitors newly created AMM pairs and liquidity pools across major DEXs (Raydium, Uniswap, GeckoTerminal). Surfaces new pools within minutes of creation, helping traders discover early opportunities.
